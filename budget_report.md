## Tips for Saving Money and Group Deals
- Look for group discounts on activities; many water sports offer discounts for groups.
- Eat at local eateries instead of tourist spots to save on food costs.
- Rent a scooter for transportation; it’s cheaper than taxis and allows for more exploration.
- Take advantage of free attractions like beaches and local festivals.
